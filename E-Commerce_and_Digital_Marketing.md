## 1. E-Commerce
**Objective:** create an online presence so customers can discover, evaluate, purchase, and return to the brand.  

**Customer journey:** Discover → Consider → Buy → Come Back

**Five digital marketing channels:** SEO, SEM, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Social Media Marketing

## 2. Why Does E-Commerce Matter?
Large, growing market; hundreds of millions of active online shoppers; quick-commerce speed/convenience.
> **Attention is the new shelf space.**
**Attention → Awareness → Consideration → Purchase → Repeat Purchase**

## 3. The Five Channels

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** organic visibility, free/earned traffic, product discovery, compounds over time (blogs/guides).

- **SEO → GEO:** the lecture's introduction of **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — optimizing for visibility inside AI-generated answers rather than just classic search rankings — reflects a real, current shift in the industry as AI search grows.

**SEM (Search Engine Marketing):** paid search visibility. **SEO = earn visibility, SEM = pay for visibility.** Fast, measurable, good for launches/time-bound sales.

**Content Marketing:** useful/entertaining/educational content that pulls people toward the brand — builds trust, educates, reduces uncertainty/returns, compounds over time.

**Email Marketing:** targeted, permission-based comms. Useful for personalization, retention, abandoned-cart recovery.[^1]

Types: Welcome, Offers, Brand story, Reminders, Updates, Transactions, FOMO, Personalized.

**Email = relationship + retention + repeat purchase**

**Social Media Marketing:** discovery, awareness, community, engagement, creators/influencers, paid ads, product discovery, sales.

**Attention → Awareness → Interest → Community → Purchase**

## 4. How the Channels Work Together
| Channel | Main Role | Speed | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Awareness → Consideration | Slow | Long-term organic traffic |
| SEM | Consideration → Purchase | Fast | Paid, measurable traffic |
| Content | Awareness → Trust | Slow | Education + authority |
| Email | Purchase → Retention | Fast | Relationship + loyalty |
| Social | Awareness → Community | Fast | Brand voice + visibility |

**SEO + Content → Earn attention | SEM + Social → Capture attention | Email → Keep the relationship alive**

**Overall: Attract → Engage → Convert → Retain**

## 3. SEO
**Checklist:** right keywords/intent, fast mobile-friendly pages, clear titles/meta descriptions, quality backlinks, fresh content.  

**Nykaa:** long-form guides, ingredient explainers, listicles — answer the real question first, don't keyword-stuff.  

**SEO in 2026:** **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** and **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — becoming a citable source for AI/search experiences, not just ranking. *(Both terms reflect a real and current shift in SEO practice as AI-generated answers increasingly mediate search.)*

## 4. SEM
Paid ads on search engines/marketplaces (Google Ads, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra). Instant visibility, measurable, good for launches/sales, targets existing intent.  

**SEO = earn visibility, SEM = pay for visibility**  

**boAt case:** festive-sale sponsored ads + retail-media placements around high-intent searches.  

**Checklist:** clear goal, keyword targeting, negative keywords[^2], landing page matches ad promise, monitor budget/bids.  

**Retail Media:** advertising directly on marketplaces/quick-commerce apps where intent is already high (Amazon Ads, Flipkart Ads, Blinkit Ads).

## 5. Content Marketing
Useful/entertaining content (video, blogs, guides, reels) that pulls people in by helping/entertaining first.  

**Mamaearth:** ingredient-education content on YouTube/Instagram — answers real questions before pushing product.  

**Checklist:** solve a real question first, native formats per platform, consistent cadence, repurpose one asset into many formats, track saves/shares (not just likes).  

**2026 shift:** AI-assisted content creation, UGC, creator-economy investment, short-form video, more human/imperfect-looking content.

## 6. Email Marketing
Targeted, permission-based emails; brand owns the subscriber relationship.

**Lifecycle:** Welcome → Browse/Cart abandonment → Post-purchase → Win-back

**Myntra:** behaviour-triggered flows (cart abandonment, post-purchase).   
Key learning: **Right trigger + Right message + Right moment.**

**Checklist:** clear consent at signup, welcome series, one trigger per key event, concise subject lines, easy unsubscribe.

**2026 shift:** AI-powered personalization, interactive email elements, behaviour-based messaging, data privacy/consent requirements.

## 7. Social Media Marketing
Building presence/sales via Instagram, YouTube, X — organic content, influencer partnerships, paid social.  

**Zepto & Blinkit:** witty, meme-fluent, culturally reactive social presence — real, verifiable brand positioning for both.  

**Checklist:** consistent brand voice, react to culture quickly, mix organic + paid, track shares/saves, keep a human presence behind the account.  

**Social Commerce:** Creators → Content → Discovery → Trust → Purchase — creators moving from ad space to part of the selling experience itself.

## 8. Putting the Five Channels Together
| Channel | Funnel Stage | Speed | Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Awareness → Consideration | Slow | Low, ongoing | Long-term organic traffic |
| SEM | Consideration → Purchase | Instant | Pay-per-click | Launches, sales events |
| Content | Awareness → Trust | Slow | Low–Medium | Education, brand trust |
| Email | Purchase → Retention | Fast | Very low | Cart recovery, loyalty |
| Social | Awareness → Community | Fast | Low–High | Brand voice, virality |

**SEO + Content → Earn attention | SEM + Social → Capture attention | Email → Keep the relationship alive**
**Integrated: Attract → Engage → Convert → Retain**

## 1. E-Commerce Marketing

### Customer Journey Loop

**Discover → Consider → Buy → Return**

The customer moves through different stages before and after purchase.

### E-Commerce vs Traditional Marketing

A key advantage of e-commerce marketing is **measurability** — digital activities can be tracked and measured.

---

## 2. Customer Persona

A **customer persona** is a semi-fictional representation of the ideal customer.

It helps marketers understand:
- Who the customer is
- Their needs and pain points
- Their behaviour
- What kind of marketing message may work for them

---

## 3. E-Commerce Marketing Strategy

Before creating the strategy:

1. Conduct **market research**
2. Understand the **customer**
3. Identify the **market gap**
4. Analyse **competitors**
5. Understand competitor products and positioning
6. Define the **target audience**
7. Decide the brand's **positioning**

### Target Audience & Positioning

**Target audience:** Who you want to reach.

**Positioning:** How you want the brand/product to be perceived in the market.

---

## 4. SMART Goals

Marketing goals should be:

- **Specific**
- **Measurable**
- **Achievable**
- **Relevant**
- **Time-bound**

Goals should be measurable so that performance can be tracked.

---

## 5. Choosing Marketing Channels

Marketing channels should be selected based on:

**The customer + the funnel stage + where the audience is**

Do not choose a channel only because it is currently trending.

### Funnel-Based Channel Selection

- **Awareness:** Reach / Impressions
- **Consideration:** CTR
- **Purchase:** Conversion Rate
- **Retention:** Repeat Purchase

---

## 6. E-Commerce Marketing Plan

The lecture discussed a mix of different execution areas:

### Performance Marketing
- Paid
- Fast
- Focused on driving measurable results

### Content Marketing
- Builds trust
- Helps customers understand the product

### Social Commerce
- Helps customers discover and buy through social platforms

### Influencer Marketing
- Helps create awareness and consideration

### Email & WhatsApp
- Useful for retention
- Relatively inexpensive
- Can be effective for bringing customers back

### Marketplace Marketing
- Helps products gain visibility on e-commerce marketplaces

> **Key idea:** A strong e-commerce marketing plan uses a **mix of channels**, rather than depending on only one.

## 8. Execution of an E-Commerce Marketing Plan

The plan should connect:

**Goal → Audience → Channel → Execution → Measurement → Optimization**

The performance of each activity should be monitored so that budget and effort can be adjusted.

## 9. E-Commerce Evolution

The lecture discussed the broad evolution of e-commerce:

| Period | Major Development |
|---|---|
| 1994–2000 | Desktop |
| 2007–2012 | Mobile |

### Broadcaster vs Measurable

Traditional marketing is largely **broadcast-oriented**.

E-commerce and digital marketing allow marketers to **measure user behaviour, responses and outcomes**.

---

## 10. Types of E-Commerce

### B2C — Business to Consumer
Business sells directly to consumers.

**Example:** Amazon

### B2B — Business to Business
One business sells products/services to another business.

**Example:** IndiaMART

### C2C — Consumer to Consumer
Consumers sell to other consumers.

**Example:** OLX

### D2C — Direct to Consumer
A brand sells directly to customers through its own website or direct channel.

**Example:** Brand-owned website

### Social Commerce

Selling or enabling shopping through social platforms.

**Examples:** Instagram Shop, TikTok Shop
