## 3. Promptomania — Prompt Structure for Logo Design

Promptomania was discussed as a prompt-building resource for image generation.

A structured logo prompt can include four key elements:

### 1. Business Type

Tell AI what the company does.

Examples:
- Coffee shop
- Technology startup
- Fashion brand
- Real estate company
- Fitness studio

### 2. Logo Style

Define the visual direction.

Examples:
- Minimalist
- Luxury
- Modern
- Vintage
- Futuristic
- Playful

### 3. Symbol or Icon

Suggest meaningful visual elements.

Examples:
- Mountain
- Leaf
- Shield
- Coffee bean
- Digital circuit
- Animal mascot

### 4. Color Palette

Colors influence brand perception.

Examples:
- Black and gold — luxury
- Blue — trust
- Green — sustainability
- Orange — energy

## 4. Other Logo Prompt Inputs

A logo prompt can also specify:

- Brand personality
- Visual style
- Color preference
- Target audience
- Design direction

### Structured Logo Prompt

> “Design a [Style / Aesthetic] logo for [Brand Name], a [Type of Business / Industry] aimed at [Target Audience]. The design should feature [Core Visual Elements / Symbols], using a [Color Palette] color scheme. Use [Font Style / Typography] typography. The logo must convey [Brand Values / Emotions] and be suitable for [Primary Application, e.g. app icon, business cards, signage].”

## 10. AI Portrait Prompt Types

Several practical portrait prompt formats were demonstrated.

### 1. Professional Headshot

> Create a realistic professional headshot of [person description]. Preserve natural facial proportions, skin texture and distinctive features. Use soft window light from the left, a neutral studio background, subtle depth of field and a polished editorial photography look.

### 2. LinkedIn Profile Photo

> Turn this reference photo into a professional LinkedIn portrait. Preserve the person's exact identity, face shape, hairline and recognizable features. Use clean studio lighting, a softly blurred neutral background and business-casual clothing.

### 3. Editorial Fashion Portrait

> Create a high-end editorial fashion portrait of [person] wearing [outfit]. Use three-quarter framing, dramatic side lighting, a textured studio backdrop, subtle film grain and magazine-quality color grading.

### 4. Natural Lifestyle Portrait

> Create a candid lifestyle portrait of [person] in [location]. Use natural daylight, realistic skin texture, imperfect hair strands and authentic body posture. Make it feel like a professionally photographed real moment.

### 5. Cinematic Character Portrait

> Create a cinematic close-up of [character/person] in [environment]. Use practical lighting from [light source], atmospheric depth, realistic skin and fabric textures and shallow depth of field. Keep anatomy natural.

### 6. Corporate Team Portrait

> Create a realistic corporate team photograph with [number] people from [company/team]. Arrange everyone naturally with believable spacing and varied expressions. Use soft office lighting and realistic skin tones.

## 11. General Prompt Structure

A useful structured prompt can specify:

**Subject / Person → Context / Business → Style → Visual Elements → Color → Lighting → Composition → Intended Application**

The more clearly the desired visual direction is described, the more specific the generated output can be.

## Lecture 2
**Date:** 21 September 2026

## 1. AI for SEO

The lecture introduced practical ways AI can be used for SEO research,
audits, content planning and competitive analysis.

### SEO Audit

The main audit areas were:

-   Technical & Indexability Health
-   On-Page & Heading Structure
-   Search Intent Alignment
-   Internal & External Linking
-   Prioritized Action Plan

The prioritized action plan asks for the top 10 critical issues, their
traffic/ranking impact, priority level (High/Medium/Low), and
step-by-step fixes.

## 2. Technical SEO Analysis

AI can analyse page speed metrics, mobile-friendliness, indexability
issues and site architecture, and provide recommendations.

## 3. Content Gap Analysis

AI can compare a client website with competitors to identify content
gaps, keyword opportunities and topics that could drive more organic
traffic.

## 4. Backlink Profile Audit

Examples included:

-   Identifying toxic links
-   Comparing backlink profiles with competitors
-   Finding quality link-building opportunities
-   Finding websites linking to multiple competitors but not the client
-   Evaluating anchor-text distribution and possible over-optimization

## 5. On-Page SEO Assessment

AI can analyse title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, content
quality and keyword usage for specific pages.

## 6. Local SEO Audit

Areas included Google Business Profile optimization, local citations,
review management, local link opportunities, NAP consistency, and
comparison of local ranking factors.

## 7. E-commerce SEO Audit

AI can analyse product-page optimization, category structure, schema
markup, user experience, product schema and internal linking between
categories and products.

## 8. Mobile SEO Analysis

AI can analyse mobile usability, responsive design, mobile page speed,
mobile-specific ranking factors, mobile vs desktop performance, and
mobile UX issues.

## 9. Core Web Vitals Analysis

The lecture included prompts for analysing LCP, FID and CLS, comparing
them with industry benchmarks, identifying problematic JavaScript/CSS,
and comparing performance with competitors.

## 10. Content Quality Assessment

AI can evaluate content using E-A-T principles, readability, depth and
relevance to search intent. Examples included identifying thin content
and comparing readability with competitors.

## 11. International SEO Audit

AI can analyse international SEO implementation, geo-targeting,
international backlinks, localization, language targeting and
performance across country versions.

## 12. Competitor SEO Strategy Analysis

AI can analyse competitor keywords, content types, backlink sources,
technical SEO approaches, publication frequency and topical focus.

## 13. Schema Markup Audit

AI can audit structured data for missing markup, errors and rich-result
opportunities, including product and FAQ schema.

## 14. Seasonal Keyword Opportunities

AI can identify:

-   Keywords that spike in specific months
-   Events, holidays and industry cycles driving search
-   When content should be published before a seasonal peak
-   Seasonal keywords with year-round value

The output can be organised as a 12-month content calendar.

## 15. Content Brief Creation

A content brief prompt can include:

-   Primary keyword
-   5--8 secondary keywords
-   Search intent
-   Title under 60 characters
-   Meta description under 155 characters
-   URL slug
-   Recommended word count
-   H2/H3 outline
-   People Also Ask questions
-   Internal linking suggestions
-   External source types
-   Differentiated content angle

## 16. Title Tag Generation

The lecture prompt asks for 5 title-tag options that:

-   Stay under 60 characters
-   Put the primary keyword within the first 40 characters
-   Include a reason to click
-   Avoid clickbait or misleading framing

# AI Product Photography Prompts

## 18. Pure White Marketplace Packshot

> Place the \[product\] centered on a pure white background, keep the
> exact shape, proportions, branding, label text, and material finish,
> use soft studio lighting, realistic shadow directly under the product,
> e-commerce packshot style, no props, no extra objects.

## 19. Soft Gray Studio Hero Shot

> Place the \[product\] in a clean studio setup with a light gray
> seamless background, soft diffused lighting, subtle natural shadow,
> product fully visible, sharp edges, realistic reflections if the
> material is glossy, premium e-commerce photography.

## 20. Open-and-Closed View

> Show the \[product\] in both closed and open state in one clean
> composition, preserve accurate design details, simple studio
> background, realistic shadow, e-commerce comparison layout.

## 21. Floating Cutout with Clean Shadow

> Turn the \[product\] into a floating studio product image on a clean
> neutral background, preserve exact product geometry, add a soft shadow
> below to ground it, keep all visible details realistic and accurate.

## 22. Top-Down Flat Lay

> Create a top-down flat lay of the \[product\] on a clean studio
> surface, balanced composition, soft even lighting, realistic material
> texture, minimal shadow, commercial product photography style.

## 23. Natural Habitat Scene

> Place the \[product\] in its natural real-life environment, styled
> realistically, keep the exact product design, branding, and
> proportions, use believable lighting and shadows, make the scene look
> like authentic commercial photography.

## 24. Minimal Interior Scene

> Place the \[product\] in a modern minimal interior, clean composition,
> neutral styling, realistic daylight, the product remains the hero, no
> clutter, editorial e-commerce photography.

## 25. Outdoor Lifestyle Scene

> Place the \[product\] in an outdoor setting that matches its use,
> natural light, realistic environment, accurate texture and color,
> product clearly visible, premium lifestyle product photography.

## 26. Hands-in-Frame Usage Shot

> Show the \[product\] being used by hands only, no full person visible,
> realistic interaction, clean composition, natural lighting, keep the
> product details accurate and in focus.

## 27. Desk Setup

> Place the \[product\] in a realistic desk setup with complementary
> objects, clean arrangement, soft window light, keep the product as the
> focal point, modern commercial photography.

## 28. Material Texture Close-Up

> Create a close-up detail image of the \[product\] focused on material
> texture, stitching, finish, or surface quality, realistic macro-style
> lighting, sharp detail, premium product photography.

## 29. In-Hand Size Reference

> Show the \[product\] being held naturally in one hand to communicate
> scale, realistic proportions, clean background, clear focus on the
> product, no distortion, e-commerce lifestyle photography.

## 30. Summer Campaign Version

> Place the \[product\] in a bright summer setting, warm natural light,
> fresh energetic atmosphere, clean composition, realistic shadows, keep
> the product shape, branding, and details exact.

## 31. Model Using the Product Naturally

> Show a model naturally using the \[product\] in a realistic setting,
> keep the product fully accurate in shape, size, and branding, product
> clearly visible, natural pose, commercial lifestyle photography.

## 32. Black Friday / Cyber Monday

> Place the \[product\] in a bold Black Friday themed campaign scene,
> strong commercial composition, high-contrast lighting, modern
> promotional feel, keep the product shape, branding, and details exact.
