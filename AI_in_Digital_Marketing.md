# Artificial Intelligence in Digital Marketing

**Credits:** 3  
**Semester:** III  
**Program:** Post Graduate Program in Digital Media and Marketing Communication

## 1. Course Overview

This course introduces Artificial Intelligence (AI), its core concepts and its applications in digital marketing. It covers AI tools used by digital marketers, applications such as personalization and predictive analytics, chatbots, programmatic advertising, content creation and ethical practices in AI.

### Pre-requisite

Basic understanding of SEO, SEM, content marketing and social media marketing.

## 2. Course Objectives

- Build a foundation in the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence.
- Understand various AI subfields.
- Understand the strengths and limitations of different AI algorithms.
- Learn how to choose the right AI tool for the job.
- Understand applications of AI in digital marketing.

## 3. Learning Outcomes

After completion of the course, students would be able to:

1. Understand how Artificial Intelligence is important in digital marketing.
2. Apply different types of Artificial Intelligence tools used for digital marketers.
3. Analyze various Artificial Intelligence application tools.
4. Create the basics of Artificial Intelligence chatbots.
