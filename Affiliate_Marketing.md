# Affiliate Marketing

**Credits:** 3  
**Semester:** III  
**Program:** Post Graduate Program in Digital Media and Marketing Communication

## 1. Course Overview

Affiliate Marketing focuses on the key components of affiliate marketing, including affiliates, merchants, networks and commissions. The course also covers the benefits of affiliate marketing and different types of affiliate programs and their suitability for products and audiences.

### Pre-requisite

Basic understanding pertaining to digital marketing concepts.

## 2. Course Objectives

- Understand the key components of affiliate marketing.
- Understand affiliates, merchants, networks and commissions.
- State the benefits of affiliate marketing for businesses and affiliates.
- Discuss different affiliate programs such as CPA and CPL.
- Understand the suitability of different affiliate programs for products and audiences.

## 3. Learning Outcomes

After completion of the course, students would be able to:

1. Understand different types of affiliate marketing.
2. Apply various affiliate marketing tools.
3. Analyze different types of affiliate programs and marketing strategies.
4. Formulate different methods of generating a profitable affiliate marketing business.
