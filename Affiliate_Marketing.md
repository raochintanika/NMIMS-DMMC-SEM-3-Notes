## 1. Affiliate Marketing

**Affiliate Marketing** is a **performance-based marketing partnership** where an affiliate promotes a merchant's product/service and earns a **commission based on customer actions** such as clicks, leads, or purchases.

**Key idea:** Payment is based on performance.

### Why Affiliate Marketing Works

- Affiliate activity is **trackable**
- Clicks, leads and purchases can be measured
- Merchants can extend their reach through affiliate networks
- It can be a relatively **low-risk and cost-effective growth channel**

## 2. Customer Journey Loop

**Discover → Consider → Buy → Return**

Affiliate marketing can influence the customer at different stages of this journey.

## 3. Three-Way / Multi-Party Relationship

The core relationship involves:

- **Merchant / Advertiser** — has the product or service and runs the affiliate programme
- **Affiliate / Publisher** — promotes the product or service
- **Consumer** — takes the action

An **Affiliate Network / Platform** may also support the programme by helping with tracking and partnerships.

### Important

The **merchant pays the commission**, not the social platform where the affiliate publishes the content.

## 4. How Affiliate Marketing Works

1. **Merchant creates an affiliate programme**
2. Merchant provides **unique links or codes**
3. **Affiliate promotes** the product/service
4. **Consumer takes an action**
5. The action is **tracked**
6. Affiliate receives the agreed **commission**

> **Unique links + tracking = measurable performance**

## 5. Affiliates & Engagement

Having a large follower count is **not the only benchmark**.

Merchants also look at:

- Engagement with followers
- Audience interaction
- Relevance to the brand/category
- Ability to generate actual customer action

A smaller creator with a highly engaged audience can be more valuable than a larger creator with low engagement.

### Class Example

A recipe creator with an engaged audience can promote multiple food-related brands through recipe content. The audience may click, enquire or purchase through the affiliate links.

## 6. Commission Models

### Pay Per Sale (PPS)

Affiliate earns when the customer **completes a purchase**.

**Best suited to:** Mass-consumption products and products where sales can be directly tracked.

### Pay Per Lead (PPL)

Affiliate earns when the customer **completes a target lead action**, such as filling a form or submitting an enquiry.

### Pay Per Click (PPC)

Affiliate earns based on **customer clicks** through the affiliate link.

**Class observation:** Beauty, fashion and other high-end products may use click-based affiliate models.

## 7. Tracking & Affiliate Links

Each affiliate receives a **unique link/code** so the merchant can identify the source of the action.

Different relationships can have different tracking links, depending on whether the agreed model is based on:

- Clicks
- Leads
- Sales

### Cookie Duration

Affiliate links can have a **tracking duration/cookie duration**.

This helps determine how long a customer's activity can be attributed to the affiliate.

> **Without proper tracking, commission disputes can occur.**

## 8. Popular Affiliate Channels

- **Content & review websites**
- **Influencers & social media**
- **Coupon & deal websites**
- **Email marketing** to subscribers
- **Blogs and product reviews**
- **Vlogs and unboxing videos**
- **Live streaming**

### Content-Based Affiliate Marketing

Affiliates can create:

- Product reviews
- Blogs
- Unboxing content
- Product comparisons
- Recipe/content integrations

The content helps create **trust → discovery → action**.

## 9. Affiliate Marketing as a Side Income

Affiliate marketing can be used as a **side gig**.

It offers flexibility because affiliates can:

- Choose their niche
- Choose the platform they are comfortable with
- Create content around their expertise
- Build their own audience/network

> **Create content → Build an audience → Generate actions → Earn commission**

## 10. Advantages & Challenges

### Advantages

- Performance-based
- Trackable
- Relatively low financial risk for merchants
- Extends the merchant's reach
- Flexible income opportunity for affiliates
- Can support business growth

### Challenges

- High competition
- Income is not always stable
- Commission structures can change
- Payment delays may occur
- Fake/fraudulent links can create problems
- Affiliates can lose credibility if they promote products carelessly

## 11. Credibility & Responsible Promotion

Affiliate marketing depends heavily on **trust**.

Affiliates should be careful about:

- What products they promote
- Whether their claims are truthful
- Maintaining audience trust
- Disclosing compensated/affiliate relationships where required

The lecture discussed how poorly considered promotions can damage an affiliate's credibility.

## 12. Affiliate Marketing & Compliance

Affiliate marketing involves **consumer protection and disclosure considerations**.

Where an affiliate receives financial compensation for promotion, appropriate **disclosure** may be required.

The lecture also highlighted the importance of:

- Honest claims
- Actual product experience where relevant
- Clear affiliate disclosures
- Proper tracking links
- Privacy/data considerations

> **Performance marketing still needs responsible and compliant marketing practices.**

## 13. Affiliate Networks & E-Commerce Platforms

Affiliate networks can help merchants:

- Find potential affiliates
- Manage affiliate programmes
- Track activity
- Coordinate partnerships

The lecture highlighted the importance of e-commerce platforms and affiliate networks in expanding the reach of merchants.

## 14. Indian Affiliate Marketing Examples

The lecture discussed examples of affiliates earning through:

- Beauty and fashion
- Food and recipe content
- E-commerce products
- Wealth-management/financial platforms
- Cashback and deal platforms

A key observation was that **tier-2 and tier-3 audiences** can be important for affiliate-driven commerce, particularly in categories such as clothing, jewellery and household products.

## 15. Affiliate Marketing for Brands

For a merchant, affiliate marketing can act as a **growth channel** without requiring the brand to reach every consumer directly.

**Merchant → Affiliate Network → Affiliates → Consumers**

The merchant can track:

**Click → Lead → Purchase**

This data helps the merchant understand which affiliates and activities are generating results.

## Quick Revision — Lecture 1

**Affiliate Marketing:** Performance-based marketing partnership

**Core parties:** Merchant + Affiliate + Consumer (+ Affiliate Network/Platform)

**How it works:** Programme → Unique Link/Code → Promotion → Customer Action → Tracking → Commission

**Commission models:** PPS → Pay Per Sale | PPL → Pay Per Lead | PPC → Pay Per Click

**Affiliate benchmark:** Engagement matters, not just follower count

**Channels:** Reviews | Blogs | Influencers | Social | Coupons | Email | Videos | Live streams

**Key advantage:** Trackable + performance-based + measurable

**Key challenge:** Competition + unstable income + credibility + compliance

**Core idea:** **Right affiliate + right audience + right tracking = measurable growth**
