# Affiliate Marketing — Quiz

### Lecture 1 (12 September 2026)

Q1. How is Affiliate Marketing defined in the lecture?

A. A fixed-fee advertising arrangement regardless of performance
B. A performance-based marketing partnership where affiliates earn commission based on customer actions
C. A form of traditional broadcast advertising
D. A subscription-based content model

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Affiliate Marketing is defined as a performance-based marketing partnership where an affiliate earns a commission based on customer actions such as clicks, leads, or purchases.

Q2. In the three-way affiliate relationship, who pays the commission?

A. The social media platform where content is published
B. The consumer
C. The Merchant/Advertiser
D. The Affiliate Network only

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The lecture explicitly notes that the merchant pays the commission, not the social platform where the affiliate publishes content.

Q3. Which commission model is best suited for high-end products like beauty or fashion, according to the class observation?

A. Pay Per Sale (PPS)
B. Pay Per Lead (PPL)
C. Pay Per Click (PPC)
D. Pay Per Impression

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The class observation notes that beauty, fashion and other high-end products may use click-based (PPC) affiliate models.

Q4. According to the lecture, what benchmark matters more than follower count when evaluating an affiliate?

A. The affiliate's number of years active
B. Engagement with followers and ability to generate customer action
C. The affiliate's location
D. The affiliate's number of past brand partnerships

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Merchants look beyond follower count to engagement, audience interaction, relevance, and ability to generate actual customer action — a smaller, highly engaged creator can be more valuable than a larger one with low engagement.

Q5. What role does "cookie duration" play in affiliate tracking, according to the lecture?

A. It determines the affiliate's payment currency
B. It determines how long a customer's activity can be attributed to the affiliate
C. It sets the maximum number of affiliates per merchant
D. It has no impact on commission disputes

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Cookie duration (tracking duration) determines how long a customer's activity can be attributed to the affiliate; without proper tracking, commission disputes can occur.

Q6. Which of the following platforms was NOT mentioned in the lecture's list of affiliate networks/e-commerce platforms discussed?

A. Amazon Associates
B. EarnKaro
C. Shopify
D. LinkedIn Ads

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The lecture lists Amazon Associates, Flipkart, Meesho, Shopify, EarnKaro, and CashKaro as platforms/examples discussed — LinkedIn Ads was not mentioned in this context.
