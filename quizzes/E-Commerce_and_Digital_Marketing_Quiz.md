# E-Commerce & Digital Marketing — Quiz

### Lecture 1 (22 August 2026)

Q1. According to the lecture, what are the five digital marketing channels discussed for e-commerce?

A. SEO, SEM, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Social Media Marketing
B. SEO, PR, Affiliate Marketing, Direct Mail, TV
C. SEM, Display, OTT, Radio, Print
D. Content, Influencer, Retail Media, Affiliate, SMS

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The lecture explicitly lists SEO, SEM, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, and Social Media Marketing as the five channels.

Q2. What is the key difference between SEO and SEM highlighted in class?

A. SEO is paid visibility; SEM is organic visibility
B. SEO = earn visibility; SEM = pay for visibility
C. SEO and SEM are the same thing
D. SEM is slower than SEO

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The lecture defines SEO as earning visibility (organic) and SEM as paying for visibility (paid search).

Q3. In the Nykaa case study, what approach did the lecture highlight for driving product discovery?

A. Heavy paid advertising with minimal content
B. Product guides and educational content leading to discovery
C. Exclusive reliance on influencer marketing
D. Cold email campaigns only

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Nykaa case study highlighted product guides and educational content in the flow: Search → Educational Content → Product Discovery → Consideration → Purchase.

Q4. According to the lecture, what is the overall customer journey model for e-commerce?

A. Attract → Convert → Retain
B. Discover → Consider → Buy → Come Back
C. Awareness → Interest → Desire → Action
D. Plan → Reach → Act → Convert

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The e-commerce customer journey discussed is Discover → Consider → Buy → Come Back.

Q5. Which statement best reflects the lecture's framing of "Attention is the new shelf space"?

A. Physical retail shelf space no longer matters at all
B. Capturing consumer attention online is analogous to securing shelf space in traditional retail
C. Only paid ads can capture consumer attention online
D. Shelf space is irrelevant to digital marketing

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The lecture frames attention as the modern equivalent of retail shelf space, driving the flow Attention → Awareness → Consideration → Purchase.

Q6. What is the primary strength of Email Marketing, according to the "How the Channels Work Together" table?

A. Long-term organic traffic
B. Education and authority
C. Relationship and loyalty
D. Brand voice and visibility

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Email's main role is Purchase → Retention, with its strength being relationship and loyalty, distinct from SEO's organic traffic or Social's brand voice.

### Lecture 2 (29 August 2026)

Q1. Which e-commerce segment do Blinkit, Zepto, and Instamart belong to, according to the lecture?

A. Horizontal Marketplaces
B. Quick Commerce
C. D2C & Social Commerce
D. Value & Reseller Commerce

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart and Amazon Now are categorised under Quick Commerce, characterised by fast delivery and growing ad revenue.

Q2. What new concept did the lecture introduce as part of SEO's evolution in 2026?

A. CRM Optimization
B. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
C. CPM Bidding Strategy
D. Retail Media Auctions

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The lecture introduced GEO and AEO — optimizing for visibility inside AI-generated answers, becoming a citable source, not just ranking in classic search.

Q3. In the Myntra example under Email Marketing, what key learning was emphasized?

A. Send as many emails as possible to maximise reach
B. Right trigger + Right message + Right moment
C. Email marketing is less effective than SEM
D. Avoid behaviour-triggered flows

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Myntra's behaviour-triggered flows (cart abandonment, post-purchase) illustrated the principle: Right trigger + Right message + Right moment.

Q4. According to the lecture, what does India's DPDP Act relate to in the context of digital marketing?

A. Retail media auction rules
B. Consent becoming part of marketing execution, not just a legal formality
C. SEO ranking algorithms
D. Quick-commerce delivery regulations

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The lecture discussed the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act as making consent an operational part of marketing execution, not merely a legal box to tick.

Q5. Which career path is associated with the "Content" channel in the lecture's career opportunities list?

A. PPC Analyst
B. Content Marketing Manager
C. CRM/Lifecycle Marketing Manager
D. Social Media Manager

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Content Marketing was linked to roles such as Content Marketing Manager, Brand Storyteller, and Creator Partnerships Lead.

Q6. In the Mamaearth integrated example, which combination of channels was used?

A. Only SEO and Content
B. SEO + SEM + Content + Email + Social
C. Only Social Media and Email
D. SEM and Affiliate Marketing only

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Mamaearth's integrated example combined SEO (ingredient blogs), SEM (sponsored placements), Content (YouTube/Insta education), Email (replenishment reminders), and Social (influencer/UGC community).

### Lecture 3 (12 September 2026)

Q1. What does the "S" in SMART goals stand for, according to the lecture?

A. Sustainable
B. Specific
C. Strategic
D. Social

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** SMART goals are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Q2. According to the lecture's example budget allocation, which category received the largest share?

A. Content & Influencer Marketing
B. Retention: Email & WhatsApp
C. Performance Marketing
D. Testing: New Channels & New Creatives

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The example allocation gave 50% to Performance Marketing — the largest share — followed by 25% Content & Influencer, 15% Retention, and 10% Testing.

Q3. Which e-commerce type does IndiaMART represent, according to the lecture's examples?

A. B2C
B. B2B
C. C2C
D. D2C

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** IndiaMART was given as the example for B2B (Business to Business) e-commerce, where one business sells to another.

Q4. According to the lecture's funnel-based channel selection, which metric corresponds to the "Consideration" stage?

A. Reach/Impressions
B. CTR
C. Conversion Rate
D. Repeat Purchase

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The funnel-based KPIs are Awareness → Reach/Impressions, Consideration → CTR, Purchase → Conversion Rate, Retention → Repeat Purchase.

Q5. What is described as a key advantage of e-commerce marketing over traditional marketing?

A. Higher production costs
B. Measurability — digital activities can be tracked and measured
C. Guaranteed higher sales
D. Simpler creative requirements

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The lecture contrasts traditional marketing as broadcast-oriented with e-commerce/digital marketing's ability to measure user behaviour, responses and outcomes.

Q6. According to the E-Commerce Evolution timeline discussed in class, which period is associated with "Quick Commerce" as the major development?

A. 1994–2000
B. 2007–2012
C. 2020–2023
D. 2024–2026

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The timeline lists 2020–2023 as the Quick Commerce era, following 2016–2019 (E-Commerce) and preceding 2024–2026 (AI).
