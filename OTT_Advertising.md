## 1. OTT

**OTT = Over The Top** — services delivered over the internet.

- Netflix was used as an example.
- OTT is a **non-linear medium**.
- DTH = network/signal/satellite
- Cable = cable
- OTT = internet

## 2. Traditional TV vs OTT

### Traditional TV
- Fixed schedule / EPG
- Local or national
- Family watching / Group Comsumption
- Doesn't Track metrics
- Geographic limitations
- Linear

### OTT
- On demand
- Start of Global content/
- Individual consumption
- No geography boundary
- Non-linear
- Track Metrics

## 7. Traditional TV vs OTT — Positioning in Advertisement

| Traditional TV | OTT |
|---|---|
| Limited personalization | No/less limited personalization |
| Linear | Non-linear |
| Mass audience | Target audience |
| Fixed inventory | Flexible inventory |
| Fixed infrastructure | Portable infrastructure |

## 8. OTT Monetization Models

### TVOD — Transactional Video on Demand
- Transactional video On demand
- Rent a movie (Limited)
- New Released Movies Early Access
- No ads / premium movie access

### AVOD — Advertising Video on Demand
- Advertising-supported video on demand
- Ads support the model

### FVOD — Free Video on Demand
- Free content
- No Ads variants
- For sampling and increased engagement

### SVOD — Subscription Video on Demand
- Subscription-based video on demand
- OTT subscription fee
- Ads may also be included

## Quick Revision

**OTT:** Internet-delivered, non-linear, on-demand content.

**TV:** Scheduled, linear, mass-audience medium.

**TRP:** TV measurement based on sampling.

**TVOD:** Pay/rent for specific content.

**AVOD:** Advertising-supported video on demand.

**SVOD:** Subscription-based video on demand.
